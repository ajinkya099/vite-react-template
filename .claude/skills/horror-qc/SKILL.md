# Horror film critic, shot repair and production QC

## Five review passes
1. **Story only**: Does the protagonist make decisions? What does each beat cost? Is there a meaningful payoff?
2. **Picture muted**: Are threat geography, reaction timing, camera rationale and action legible without sound?
3. **Sound only**: Are distances, dialogue, breath, changes in ambience and sound shocks physically credible?
4. **Frame-step**: Find off-model face/hands, broken knees, sliding feet, prop teleportation, floaty blood, bad lighting seams, cut discontinuities.
5. **Full playback**: Does suspense build without monotonous screaming, footsteps or loud music? Is the ending emotionally consequential?
## Subjective rubric (0–5 each, not measured audience response)
Causal story; character agency; dread progression; threat reveal; camera language; performance; lighting readability; sound perspective; effects continuity; fluid movement; emotional ending. Mark `blocker` for a severe failure even if total is high.
## Issue format
`SHOT/FRAME | observed defect | why it hurts fear/clarity | cause hypothesis | owning skill | smallest repair | expected new test`.
## Hard blockers
Unsafe practice; incoherent story objective; distorted character identity; motion interpolation artifacts; unreadable key action; dialogue sync failure; no verified final media.
## Repair decision
One defective shot -> localized repair. Inconsistent design across shots -> fix bible and revalidate downstream. Broken story causality -> return to script/fear map before any rendering.
## Output
Prioritized QC report, pass/fail by gate, and validation evidence. Never mark `rendered and inspected` based solely on prompts or a storyboard.
