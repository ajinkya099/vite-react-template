## Threat rule sheet (horror-script)
- **Rule:** asks "मेरे पैर कहाँ हैं?"; a wrong answer takes your legs. She visits within three nights of hearing the story.
- **Limit:** she won't ask if she sees your face first. She cannot lie, and she trusts *announcements*.
- **Signal:** ठक… छन… (elbow on ballast + glass bangle). Nearby machines go quiet (the kettle).
- **Cost:** your legs, or the ear-side second question.
- **Loophole:** "बावन वाली पैसेंजर… प्लेटफ़ॉर्म तीन" sends her to wait for a train that never comes. Twist: it is the very wrong announcement that killed her.
- **Hidden causality (writer knows, audience learns at SCN-11):** Sudha's misread platform slip (1991) killed Chanda. Sudha's nightly 11:52 announcement is a safety ritual. Chanda mimics announcer voices because an announcement is what she followed.

## Camera + light plan (horror-camera / horror-light), per scene
- **Platform geography (locked):** platform 1 is near and runs left→right; the tea stall is at screen-left mid-platform; the stairs/FOB are at screen-right; tracks 1–3 recede in fog; platform 3 is the far island. The yellow sodium lamp (practical) is above the stall; the cold fog glow comes from far station lights. One fixed axis is kept for all of SCN-07/08.
- **SCN-01:** locked wide on the empty platform. Slow 6 s dolly toward the lamp; the whisper triggers a cut to black.
- **SCN-03:** classroom, angithi as the warm practical (flicker on the coals). Two-shot of the girls, then a slow push into Rubina for "तीन रातों…".
- **SCN-05/06:** lantern practical in the house (power cut), low warm key and deep shadows. On the glass break: a close-up of the mother's face (a decision, not a scare). Rules intercut with titled insert cards (as in the reference), in our ink style.
- **SCN-07:** wide, then a slow creep-in on Anaya against the stall. When the kettle dies, a 2-frame hold, then a rack of the fog behind her (suspense: the audience sees the shape before she does).
- **SCN-08:** the PA horn in close-up (a red tally lamp is NOT lit: the visual clue for the deduction). The ankle touch is an extreme close-up: pale fingers + a bangle at the heel, never the body. Anaya's face holds still on a locked medium. Question 1 is played on her eyes fixed on the tracks.
- **SCN-09:** the ear-side whisper is an extreme close-up of her ear; a dark shape blurs at the frame edge. The run is a side-tracking shot through the mustard fields in fog, with low handheld energy (justified: bodily danger).
- **SCN-11:** sepia flashback, younger Sudha at the mic, the slip in her hand (insert). The accident is off-screen: a light flash + sound, then a cut to the slip fluttering on the track.
- **SCN-13:** inserts: the register, the footprints + drag line ending at the edge, the signal log. Slow tilt up from the line to the empty fog.
- **SCN-14/15:** the present-day announcer cabin, adult Anaya; the PA tally lamp lights *by itself*. The outro is a locked frontal shot, with the cabin light dying at the last line.

## Production workflow (recommended)
1. **Script lock** (this checkpoint).
2. **Audio first** (AUDIO_DIRECTION.md): cast voices on Higgsfield, generate line-by-line takes, music cues and SFX, then mix the master on one 60 fps timebase. **Approval checkpoint.**
3. **Bibles:** a code-drawn model sheet for Anaya, Sudha (2011 + 1991), Rubina and Chanda (hands/silhouette only); the platform, classroom, house and cabin sets. **Approval checkpoint.**
4. **Animatic:** every shot blocked against the locked audio at low detail, full length. **Approval checkpoint.**
5. **Final render in blocks of ~60–90 s** (60 fps, 1920×1080), each validated (frame count, smoothness, determinism, A/V sync), then a combined master and QC (horror-qc five passes).

**Honest scale note:** about 11 minutes needs ~45 shots, 4 character rigs (with full-body walk/run and seated poses, not just the close-up we built), ~6 sets, and ~39,500 frames at 60 fps. The rigs make 60 fps genuinely smooth (motion is computed per frame, not interpolated). The art build is several sessions of work, so I'll deliver it block by block with approvals, as before.
