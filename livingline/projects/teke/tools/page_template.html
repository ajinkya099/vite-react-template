テケテケमेरे पैर कहाँ हैं?

The authentic Japanese legend, set in Muroran, Hokkaido, February 1995. New characters: a skeptical 16-year-old, her 9-year-old brother, and a chain letter whose last answer was washed away by snowmelt. Hindi narration; the ghost speaks only Japanese. About 10:45 at 1920×1080, 60 fps.

What's authentic, and what's ours

No real record existsTeke Teke and Kashima Reiko are Japanese urban legends. The film doesn't claim a real accident; it makes "there is no record" part of the horror.
Folk answer 1私の足はどこ? → 名神高速道路にある ("on the Meishin Expressway", 1,000 km away, so she goes to look).
Folk answer 2誰に聞いたの? → her own name, カシマレイコ, spelled 仮面の仮・死人の死・悪魔の魔 (mask, dead person, demon).
How the legend spreadChain letters (不幸の手紙): read it and she comes within days, unless you pass it on.
Ours: the smudged answerSnowmelt in the shoe locker washes out the second answer. Aoi has to rebuild it from the letter's odd heading, a librarian's half-memory and two surviving kana.
Ours: the brother, the phone, the postboxShun read the letter too, so his third night is the same night. Aoi coaches him through both answers on a pink public phone. At dawn, the drag line in the snow leads to the red postbox.
